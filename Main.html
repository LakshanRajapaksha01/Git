Switch Connects multiple devices within a local area network (LAN) and directs data packets to the correct destination. Hub A basic device that connects multiple computers in a LAN but sends data to all devices instead of a specific one. Modem Converts digital signals to analog for transmission over telephone lines (and vice versa). Repeater Amplifies and regenerates signals to extend network range. Access Point (AP) Extends wireless network coverage and connects wireless devices to a wired network. Bridge Connects two or more LAN segments and filters traffic between them. Gateway Acts as a translator between different network protocols, allowing different networks to communicate. 03.
